# A Stromatolite For My Path *Author*: Maja Glauert ![](miriam.png class=intro) > "נֵר־לְרַגְלִ֥י דְבָרֶ֑ךָ וְ֝א֗וֹר לִנְתִיבָתִֽי׃" > > -- Tehillim 119:105 ## Miriam and Rachel I embrace the idyl, I suck down the mysid, I drift the subtidal alleys, shaded by seagrass and kelp, shadowed by ignorant vast monsters, gently blown every way by the dissipating vortices cast down from the brilliant ceiling of swells. There's a distant smell of blood. A hint of overloaded salt and iron. It's the echo, the thunder to the lightning cries that rode the vortices between the corals and the stromatolites and overcame the endless rumbling of the shore. The monsters observe their rituals, but this isn't my concern. I one with the kelp and at peace. --- I'm the length of a monster's gill and the colours of the flora--yellows and lavendars and turquoises at once both camoflauge and display. Although she's a full two cycles senior, Rachel is smaller and greener. She says she hatched into a time of scarce mysids and excess cyanobacteria, when survival was dim. The stromatolites' joy is our grief. ... ## Between Precipitates and Wrack ...
